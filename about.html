Who We Are

A Ghanaian export company built on farmer partnership and traceable quality.

Natura Agrobrand connects Ghanaian agricultural production to international markets — built on farmer partnership, traceable quality, and long-term trade relationships rather than one-off transactions.

Our Story

From Ghanaian fields to global buyers

Natura Agrobrand works with smallholder farmers, aggregators and rice plantations across Ghana, bringing millet, soybeans, rice and fresh vegetables to buyers who need a dependable, quality-assured source.

The company positions itself as more than an export intermediary: sustainable farming practice, farmer training, and fair trade are treated as part of how the business operates, not a separate program.

Mission

Providing premium agricultural products while fostering sustainable farming practices and empowering local communities across Ghana.

Vision

Becoming a trusted African agricultural export company associated with quality, sustainability and ethical business practices.

Our Values
